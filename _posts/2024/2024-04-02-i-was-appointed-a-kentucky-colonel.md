On March 25, 2024, Governor Andy Beshear commissioned me a Kentucky Colonel.
The document is an honorary commission issued in the name of the Commonwealth
of Kentucky and attested by Secretary of State Michael G. Adams. It is not a
military commission or a public office.

The title had already been part of my family history: my father was also a
Kentucky Colonel. I do not have the date of his appointment, and that is not
really the point. Receiving the same commission made this one personal in a
way that a list of distinguished recipients never could.
