What I Do

I use mathematics, statistics, software, and artificial intelligence to understand complicated systems—and explain what they mean when people and institutions have to make decisions.

Explore My World

Scholarship

I write about mathematics, operations research, technology, public policy, and the places where they collide. Sometimes this results in papers. Sometimes books. Occasionally something stranger.

Teaching

I teach mathematics and statistics by starting with the problem and working backward to the machinery. Getting the answer matters. Understanding why it works matters more.

Service

Public service is where theory has to survive contact with reality. I have served in local government, the Maryland Defense Force, professional societies, and a collection of other useful places.

Games

I wrote a GURPS supplement about hurricanes. This is either game writing, disaster research, or evidence that I will turn almost anything into a project.

My Books

I have written and edited books on numerical analysis, online mathematics education, flood insurance, and military operations research. Apparently I do not believe in picking a lane.

Media Appearance

Every so often somebody asks me to explain something on television, radio, or in print. I generally say yes, because explaining complicated things is most of the fun.

Software

I have been writing software since Unix boxes were considerably more annoying. Some of it solves real problems; some of it exists because I wanted to know whether the idea would work.
